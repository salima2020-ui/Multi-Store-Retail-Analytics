## Key Insights: Macro Factors vs Weekly Sales

1. **Macro factors explain very little of weekly sales.** All correlations with `Weekly_Sales` fall between -0.11 and +0.01. Even the strongest (Unemployment, r = -0.106) explains only about 1% of the variance.

2. **Unemployment is the strongest factor.** Its correlation is negative (r = -0.106): when unemployment rises, sales dip slightly.

3. **Fuel price has almost no relationship with sales** (r = +0.009).

4. **Temperature and CPI show weak negative links** (r = -0.064 and -0.073). The effect is too small to act on.

5. **Store-level sensitivity differs from the overall trend.** The per-store analysis identifies the stores most sensitive to unemployment, which are the best candidates for targeted promotions and inventory planning.

> Note: correlation does not imply causation. Holidays, seasonality, store size and promotions likely drive sales more than these factors.
